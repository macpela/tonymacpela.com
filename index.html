— Who is Tony

Tony Macpela is an Actor, Laser/Video Designer, Interactive Designer and Technical Director. With expertise in light, sound, and video design, he creates immersive experiences across theater, VR, and interactive installations.

His work blends cutting-edge technology with dynamic storytelling, pushing the boundaries of performance and visual art — turning any space into a spectacle of light and motion.

With a fluid and innovative style, Tony pushes the boundaries of perception, creating visual stories that engage audiences emotionally and sensorially.

Interactive Installation, Sound Art, Light Art
Sonification ROOM
Sonification Room is an interactive installation that transforms light into sound. In a hazy room, beams of light and lasers intersect a network of light sensors that trigger and manipulate musical elements. Participants use mirrors to redirect the beams, activating sounds and influencing the evolving composition. Through layered interactions and shared control, the space becomes a collective instrument where movement, light, and sound shape a dynamic musical experience.
Web Interface · 3D Printing
Project: Ascend
Merging digital design with physical sculpture. Customize a mythical statue — hair, eyes, body — name it, watch it project in real-time, then hold it in your hands through 3D printing. Featured at Ars Electronica 2025.
Sensors · Projection · Live Visuals
The Space Between Us
Love does not exist in you or in me — it exists in the fragile, invisible space between us. This installation gives shape to that space, turning the physical distance between the viewer and the canvas into a visual and emotional dialogue.
Webcam · Python · Projection · Live visuals · Realtime
Ink Flow
Ink Flow borrows a body for a moment. Movement becomes ink, stillness lets it wear your face, and a clap gives the shape back to the pool. Nothing it holds is kept.
Azure Kinect · High-Powered Laser
Presence Error
An interactive audiovisual installation that captures and re-projects the human form in real time. Using Azure Kinect, the installation tracks participants and isolates only their outline —a luminous echo, projected in front of them using a high-powered laser. The result is a raw, minimal version of self: identity stripped down to form, motion, and moment. It invites the audience to question: What remains when your presence is reduced to a signal? Where do you end, and the environment begin?
Leap Motion · Gesture Detection
Holotouch
An interactive installation that uses Leap Motion to detect hand gestures, triggering real-time video projections. By recognizing specific hand signs, it creates a seamless blend of movement and digital art, offering an immersive and responsive visual experience.
