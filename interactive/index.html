Instalație interactivă · Romania

Interactive

Tony Macpela builds interactive installations: rooms and objects that change because someone entered, moved, gestured, or pressed. The public set is seven installations plus one stage piece, Noroc sa ai, whose credit line is interactive and video design. A search for interactive installation Romania or instalație interactivă should find the work by name — ASCEND, Presence Error, Sonification Room — and not a homepage whose counters were still zero when the crawler saved it.

The interfaces are not interchangeable, and the pages refuse to pretend they are. Presence Error uses Azure Kinect and a high-powered laser to keep only the outline of a body. Holotouch uses Leap Motion and hand signs to trigger video. Ink Flow uses a webcam and Python: movement becomes ink, stillness wears a face, a clap returns the shape to the pool. House Crash publishes oversized physical controls and a collapsing cityscape, and stops there. The Space Between Us turns the distance between a viewer and a canvas into the image. Sonification Room turns mirrors, beams, and light sensors into a shared instrument. Project: Ascend lets a visitor name and customize a statue, see it projected, and hold a 3D print. Featured at Ars Electronica 2025.

None of these descriptions is a manual. If the sheet does not say what a button does, the page will not invent the rule. That restraint is the difference between a case study and a doorway page stuffed with the same paragraph seven times. What repeats is the author and the city. What does not repeat is the sensing method. Hiring "an interactive something" without saying which of those methods you mean is how these pieces get blurred into a gadget demo.

O instalație interactivă semnată de Tony Macpela nu este un ecran cu un senzor generic. Poate fi Kinect și laser, Leap Motion, webcam, butoane mari, oglinzi și fascicule, sau o statuie pe care publicul o compune și apoi o primește printată. Noroc sa ai este piesa de teatru din aceeași familie: două ecrane, un automat, imagine și sunet coordonate, regia Adina Lazar. Pentru festivaluri din România, fiecare titlu de mai jos are pagină proprie, cu tehnologia scrisă pe card.
