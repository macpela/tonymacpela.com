LIGHT UP DEMO is titled as a demo. The description says Light Up Festival and a large-scale outdoor projection installation. The tag pairs mapping with "Creative." Tony Macpela is not, on this card, claiming a prize, a winner's slot, or a key visual for the whole festival. He is publishing a demo: outdoor, large, projection. That modesty is useful. Outdoor scale is a different craft from the kitchen and a cousin of the UNATC facade, but a demo is a test of whether the picture survives distance, weather, and a crowd that did not buy a ticket to sit down.

The Vimeo is the evidence of scale. The text does not give meters, lumens, or a map. Inventing them would make the page sound like a rental quote. What can be said is structural. Large and outdoor means the edit cannot depend on a spectator two meters from a cabinet, the way Noroc sa ai can. It also means the content has to read as an installation, the word he chose, not only as an advertisement on a building. The UNATC piece is a facade with a story (shuttle, UFOs). Light Up, as written, is the scale and the festival name, plus the word demo. If you need a narrative transformation of architecture, the other page is the better precedent. If you need to see him working large and outside, this is the one.
