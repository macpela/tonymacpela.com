Clovni

Video · Lighting Design · Dir. Alexandru Ivanoiu

Clovni is video and lighting design, directed by Alexandru Ivanoiu. The description Tony publishes is the show's own premise: "Clowns Clowns Clowns" starts from three months in hospitals in therapeutic clowning and brings on stage a world where failure wears the smallest possible mask. That research belongs to the production's story as printed. This page does not reassign those hospital months to the designer's biography, and it does not add scenes the paragraph does not contain.

Video plus lighting, without laser, means the mask and the failure have to be readable in picture and in illumination together. A tiny mask is a scale problem for a camera feed and for a spotlight equally. The archive does not say whether the video is live, recorded, or both. Sirene says "live visuals" and Clovni does not. The honest difference stops there. What Clovni does specify is the emotional size: failure, made small enough to wear. Lighting on Efectul is surgical for psychological tension. Lighting here has to share the stage with an image of clowning that came out of hospitals. Those are not the same plot even though both pages sit on the technical-director list as lighting credits.

Creditul pentru video și lighting design este pe același rând, regia Alexandru Ivanoiu. Premisa publicată este a spectacolului: luni în spitale, clown terapeutic, un eșec cu masca cea mai mică. Nu transformăm premisa în biografia designerului. Paginile de actor și de director tehnic rămân separate tocmai ca să nu se amestece rolurile.
