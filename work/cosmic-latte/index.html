Cosmic Latte is the credit where Tony Macpela publishes the full stack: laser, lighting, and video, directed by Ștefan Lupu. The description is a cosmic journey through space and memory, made from live laser scanning combined with pre-rendered video. That sentence is the whole technical claim. Scanning means the beam is drawn in time, in the room. Pre-rendered video means part of the cosmos already exists as a picture. Lighting is listed because the two have to sit in a designed darkness, not in a default wash.

Space-and-memory is a theme, not a venue. The archive does not name the theater. What it does name is the combination, which is rare on the rest of the list. Mostly Fine, Thanks is laser alone. Efectul is lighting alone. Sirene is video alone. Cosmic Latte is the show where those three credits are forced to be one design, so a cue is not three departments discovering each other in tech. A technical director's problem and a designer's problem are the same problem on this title, which is why the page is linked from both the laser list and the lighting list without being labeled "TD of Cosmic Latte." The sheet does not say that. It says laser, lighting, and video.
