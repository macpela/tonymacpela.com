Project: Ascend

Interactive installation · Web Interface · 3D Printing

Watch on VimeoArs Electronica 2025

Project: Ascend, as the sheet prints it, merges a digital design with a physical sculpture. A visitor customizes a mythical statue — hair, eyes, body — names it, watches the result projected in real time, and can hold it after 3D printing. The line that matters for search and for the festival is the last one: featured at Ars Electronica 2025. The festival's own page credits Tony Macpela as interactive artist and names the UNATC MA in Interactive Technologies for Performing and Media Arts. This domain and that page are the same person. The link is on this credit so the two do not drift apart.

The interaction is a sequence of choices that become an object, not a sensor reading a dance. That puts ASCEND closer to Noroc sa ai — an interface with a result — than to Presence Error, which refuses identity down to an outline. Here identity is assembled on purpose: you pick the features, you give the name, you leave with a print. The projection is the live proof that the choice landed. The print is the proof that the digital body was allowed to become matter. The portfolio does not describe the web layout button by button. The Ars text, which this page links, talks about vaporwave, self-image, and the rituals of online identity. Where the sheet is shorter, the sheet still governs what Tony claims on his own site. The festival page is the external record, not a license to add features he did not list.

ASCEND este piesa de pus lângă căutarea Ars Electronica și lângă instalație interactivă. Statuia se compune, se proiectează, se printează.
