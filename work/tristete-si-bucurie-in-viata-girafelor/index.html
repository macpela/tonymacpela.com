Tristețe și Bucurie în viața girafelor

Lighting Design — International · Dir. Edda Coza & Dan Coza

Tristețe și Bucurie în viața girafelor is the lighting credit that already names two theaters: Teatrul Mic in Bucharest and Teatrul Alexei Mateevici in Chișinău. The portfolio calls it an international co-production and describes the light as following the emotional arc of giraffes moving between sadness and joy. Directed by Edda Coza and Dan Coza, printed that way on the sheet. Tony's line is lighting design, not a performing role and not video.

Two houses means the plot has to travel. The page does not invent a touring rider, a shared dimmer count, or which city premiered. It does treat the published fact as the important one: the same lighting design is claimed across Bucharest and Chișinău. That is the closest thing in the archive to a technical-direction problem stated out loud — one design, two buildings — while the credit itself remains lighting. Efectul is the other pure lighting credit, and it is the opposite brief: a single psychological ripple, surgical and stark, with no second city mentioned. The giraffes need an arc. Efectul needs a cut.

Dacă cineva caută lighting design pe o coproducție București–Chișinău, titlul acesta este precedenta publicată, la Teatrul Mic și la Teatrul Alexei Mateevici. Nu scriem "technical director" pe afișul lor. Scriem ce scrie arhiva: lighting design, regia Edda Coza și Dan Coza, lumina urmărind arcul dintre tristețe și bucurie.

Producers comparing Tony's lighting should read Efectul and Clovni beside this and notice the spread: an international co-production, a chamber psychological piece, and a show that pairs light with video. The through-line is that the light is dramaturgy, not a service wash. The city on his contact line is still Bucharest. The second city belongs to this title alone.
