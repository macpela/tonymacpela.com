Sirene pe Dâmbovița

Video Design · Dir. Gabriel Sandu

Watch video

Sirene pe Dâmbovița is a video-design credit, not a mapping of a monument and not a lighting plot. The myth is the Sirens, the place named in the title is the river that runs through Bucharest, and the method Tony publishes is live visuals mixed with narrative projection. Directed by Gabriel Sandu. The picture has to carry story, not only decorate a beat. That is a different edit from a facade shuttle or a kitchen dance, even though the same designer signs all three.

Live visuals mean the image can answer the room on the night. Narrative projection means some of that image is already committed to the telling of the myth. The portfolio does not itemize which scenes are live and which are fixed, and this page will not invent the split. What a director can trust is the pairing itself: a poetic text, a real river in the title, and a video designer whose other theater work runs from a slot-machine cabinet to a cosmic scan. Sirene is the one that stays with a myth and a city waterway.

Pentru cine caută un video designer de teatru în București pe un spectacol cu proiecție narativă, nu pe un mapping de fațadă, creditul este video design. Regia publicată este Gabriel Sandu. Pagina nu adaugă o sală, o stagiune sau o listă de cue-uri pe care arhiva nu o tipărește. Imaginile de pe pagină sunt cadrele din sheet, cu text alternativ care spune titlul și rolul.

Put this next to Cosmic Latte if you need to see Tony working video beside laser, or next to the AproapE readings if you need projection that serves a text rather than a building. Sirene is closer to the readings in spirit — a story is being told — and closer to Cosmic Latte in apparatus, because it is filed as video design for a full performance. The hire is the video credit. Say that in the subject line, not "projection" alone, which in this city also means architectural mapping.

Sirene pe Dâmbovița is published as Video Design. Video design for a poetic reinterpretation of the Siren myth set along the Dâmbovița river — blending live visuals with narrative projection.
