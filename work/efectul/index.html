Efectul

Lighting Design · Dir. Jasmine Brooks

Efectul is lighting design for a show about the ripple effect — one action changing everything — directed by Jasmine Brooks. Tony describes the light as stark and surgical, there to underline psychological tension. There is no video line and no laser line. The instrument is brightness, edge, and what is withheld. That is a useful counterweight to Cosmic Latte, where the same designer publishes a full cosmic stack, and to the giraffes, where the light has to carry an emotional arc across two theaters.

Surgical lighting is a claim about contrast and intention, not about a particular fixture. The archive does not name a console, a gel, or a venue. A director reading this page should expect a plot that can isolate a thought the way a cut isolates a sentence, and should not expect a catalog of moving lights. The title, Efectul, already says the theme is consequence. The lighting sentence says the consequence is visible as tension, not as decoration. If the production is the Lucy Prebble play or another text, this page does not certify the script. It certifies the credit Tony printed: lighting, Jasmine Brooks, ripple, tension.
