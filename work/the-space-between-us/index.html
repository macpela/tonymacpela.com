The Space Between Us

Interactive installation · Sensors · Projection · Live Visuals

The Space Between Us states its claim in one move: love is not in you or in me, it is in the fragile invisible space between, and the installation gives that space a shape. The physical distance between the viewer and the canvas becomes a visual and emotional dialogue. Sensors, projection, and live visuals are the tools on the card. The piece is about a gap. Filling the gap with a character, a plot, or a moral would contradict the sentence Tony published.

Distance as an input is a different sensor problem from a gesture, a clap, or a button. Presence Error also measures a body, then throws away everything but the outline. This work keeps the interval. Stepping closer or further is meaningful only because the description says the distance itself is the dialogue. The archive does not specify the curve — whether nearness calms or intensifies — and an older fallback paragraph once did. The live sheet does not. This page follows the live sheet. What remains is already enough to brief a room: a viewer, a canvas, a measured gap, live picture.

Instalația răspunde la interactive installation și la relația dintre doi poli, nu la un joc și nu la un mapping. Senzori, proiecție, imagine live. Iubirea este descrisă ca spațiul dintre, iar distanța până la pânză devine dialog. Nu adăugăm o regulă de apropiere pe care sheet-ul actual nu o mai publică.

Curators who want a relational piece, rather than a spectacle of hardware, should use this title in the email. Curators who want the audience to build an object should use ASCEND. Curators who want the audience to play an instrument of light should use Sonification Room. Tony Macpela built all three. They do not substitute for each other, which is why they do not share a paragraph.
