Noroc sa ai

Interactive · Video Design · Dir. Adina Lazar

Watch on Vimeo

Noroc sa ai is the theater piece filed as interactive and video design: a digital slot machine with a main game and a matching topper, themed sounds, and coordinated visuals on two screens. Directed by Adina Lazar. It is supposed to look and feel like a real machine. That last requirement is a design constraint, not a joke. Two screens that drift out of sync, or a topper that ignores the game, break the object. The interactivity is the object's behavior, not a sensor waved at a dance.

It sits in the interactive list beside Kinect, Leap Motion, and mirrors, and it does not use any of those phrases. The interface implied by the description is the machine itself: game, topper, sound, two pictures. The archive does not say whether the audience plays or the performers do. Inventing a player would change the piece. What is safe to say is that Tony's credit covers the image and the interactive behavior of that cabinet, under Adina Lazar's direction, and that a Vimeo link is published with the stills.

Pentru un spectacol care are nevoie de un obiect digital credibil — nu de un mapping și nu de un laser de concert — creditul public este interactive și video design. Titlul este Noroc sa ai. Regia este Adina Lazar. Două ecrane trebuie să pară un singur automat, cu sunet tematic. Asta e tot brief-ul tipărit.

Compare it with House Crash if you want the other "game" in the archive: oversized buttons and a collapsing city, and almost no other words. Noroc sa ai is the articulate one. It tells you the screens, the topper, the sound, and the standard of realism. A producer who needs a believable device on stage, rather than a responsive room, should start here and then write to Bucharest with the play's title in the subject.
