Technical director

Tony Macpela lists technical director among the jobs he actually does, next to actor, laser, video, and interactive design. This page is that job. It does not award him a "TD" credit on a poster the portfolio never printed. The sheet is explicit when the role is lighting, laser, or video, and silent when it is not. Silence stays silence. What follows are the lighting-design lines, which are the closest public record of the technical work: the person who makes a picture operable in a room, not only the person who draws it.

Tristețe și Bucurie în viața girafelor is the international lighting credit: Teatrul Mic in Bucharest and Teatrul Alexei Mateevici in Chișinău, directed by Edda Coza and Dan Coza, as the portfolio prints the names. Efectul, directed by Jasmine Brooks, is lighting for a ripple effect — stark, surgical, psychological. Clovni pairs video with lighting, directed by Alexandru Ivanoiu. Cosmic Latte puts lighting on the same line as laser and video, directed by Ștefan Lupu. Four different temperatures of the same craft. None of them is labeled, on this site, as "technical director of" that production.

Why keep a page for the job if the per-show credit is lighting? Because people search technical director teatru București and lighting design with a city, and they need to see the distinction before they write. A technical director in this practice is the layer under the design credits: how laser, video, and light share a rig and a cue structure so the show can be run, not only rendered. Where the sheet says lighting, that is the evidence. Where it says only video, or only an installation, this page does not borrow the title. Those live under video and interactive.

Pentru o sală din București, lighting design și direcția tehnică nu sunt același rând de generic, chiar dacă le face aceeași persoană. Pe lucrările de mai jos Tony Macpela este publicat cu lighting design, uneori împreună cu video sau laser. Nu adăugăm "technical director" lângă un titlu doar ca să prindem o căutare. Dacă ai nevoie de direcție tehnică pe un spectacol nou, o ceri ca atare. Dacă ai nevoie de lumini pe o coproducție sau pe un spectacol de cameră, titlurile de aici sunt precedentele publicate.

The acting page stays separate so a lighting plan is not mistaken for a performance CV. Contact is still one person in Bucharest: maccpela@gmail.com, +40 720 906 907. Name the venue type if you know it — a repertory house, a studio, a touring co-production — and name the credit you want written on the poster. The giraffes co-production is the one place the archive already prints two theaters. The other lighting pages print a director and a description, and stop.
