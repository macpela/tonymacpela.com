Mostly Fine, Thanks

Laser Design · Dir. Roxana Popa

Watch on YouTube

Mostly Fine, Thanks is laser design for an intimate show about coping, and about the thin line between fine and not fine. Directed by Roxana Popa. Tony's own sentence says the laser is both spectacle and metaphor. That double duty is the whole brief. A beam that is only pretty fails the metaphor. A metaphor you cannot see fails the spectacle. Intimate scale makes the problem stricter: there is nowhere to hide a weak idea behind haze and a chorus.

Nothing on the card says the laser draws words, outlines a body, or scans a prerender. Those techniques belong to other titles — Presence Error for the outline, Cosmic Latte for the scan. Using them here would be fan fiction about his own work. What belongs here is the subject. Coping is not a lookbook. The light has to be able to mean "I'm fine" and mean the opposite without a costume change. Spectacle is still required, because he wrote the word. The YouTube document in the sheet is the place to see how those two demands were actually balanced. This text will not narrate shots it cannot verify.

Căutarea laser design spectacol pe o formă intimă, nu pe un show mare de festival, se oprește mai corect aici decât pe o firmă de tăiere. Regia este Roxana Popa. Laserul este și spectacol, și metaforă, pe un spectacol despre coping. Nu promitem un efect anume în afara frazei publicate.

If the room is small and the subject is personal, this is the laser precedent. If the room is a cosmic stack of departments, read Cosmic Latte. If the beam has to be touched and redirected by the audience, read Sonification Room, which is an installation, not this play.
