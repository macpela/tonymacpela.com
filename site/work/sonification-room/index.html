Sonification ROOM

Interactive installation · Interactive Installation, Sound Art, Light Art

Watch on YouTube

Sonification Room turns light into sound. In the published description, a hazy room is crossed by beams and lasers, light sensors trigger and bend musical elements, and people use mirrors to redirect the beams. Shared control makes the room a collective instrument. Movement, light, and sound write the piece together. Tony Macpela's credit is the interactive installation: sound art and light art on the same card. It is the clearest "audience as player" work in the archive, and it says so without borrowing the mechanics of Ink Flow or Holotouch.

The laser here is not a concert look and not a cutting tool. It is a key. If the beam misses the sensor, the note does not exist. That is why the page also sits on the laser-design list: the beam is load-bearing. Haze is mentioned because a beam you cannot see cannot be aimed with a mirror. The archive does not print the synthesizer, the sensor model, or the number of players. It does print the social rule: layered interaction and shared control. One person can aim. The composition belongs to whoever is in the room. That is a different ethic from Presence Error, where the system isolates you, and from ASCEND, where you customize an object and take it home.

Pentru o instalație interactivă în care publicul cântă cu lumina, nu doar o privește, Sonification Room este exemplul. Lasere, senzori de lumină, oglinzi, ceață. Sunetul este rezultatul gestului, nu un fundal. Pagina nu adaugă o partitură sau un festival în afara a ceea ce cardul și documentarea video spun deja.

Sonification ROOM is published as Interactive installation. Sonification Room is an interactive installation that transforms light into sound. In a hazy room, beams of light and lasers intersect a network of light sensors that trigger and manipulate musical elements. Participants use mirrors to redirect the beams, activating sounds and influencing the evolving composition. Through layered interactions and shared control, the space becomes a collective instrument where movement, light, and sound shape a dynamic musical experience.
