This Kitchen Is For Mapping

Video mapping · TKIFM, Mapping

Watch on Vimeo

This Kitchen Is For Mapping, tagged TKIFM, takes a room that already has a job — cooking, gathering — and treats every surface as a screen for a while. The published arc is domestic space, then immersive projection, then a short dance. Video mapping here is not a parliament facade and not a theater portal. The constraint is the kitchen itself: cabinets, planes, a human scale, a dance that has to read inside that scale. Tony Macpela's credit is the mapping.

A dance at the end changes the edit. Architectural mapping can loop for a crowd that walks past. A dance needs a beginning and a body. The sheet says the projections reshape the space and the project culminates in that dance. It does not name the choreographer, the music, or the address. The Vimeo is the document. What the text is willing to emphasize is the transformation of an ordinary room, which is the argument of the title: this kitchen is for mapping, meaning the room was reassigned. UNATC Invasion, by contrast, reassigns a facade to a shuttle. The kitchen reassigns a house to a picture and then to a performer. Same author, different contract with the architecture.

Pentru video mapping pe interior domestic, nu pe fațadă monumentală, TKIFM este exemplul din București al lui Tony Macpela. Proiecția schimbă bucătăria și se încheie cu un dans scurt. Nu îl transformăm într-un festival de stradă și nu îl amestecăm cu suita AproapE, care este proiecție pentru lecturi, nu pentru mobilă.

Clients who have a real room and a body, rather than a plaza, should send this URL. Clients who have a mural or a festival exterior should send 2 Mai or Light Up. The contact is still one address in Bucharest. The surface you have is the thing that chooses the page.
