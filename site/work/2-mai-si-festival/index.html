2 Mai-Și Festival is published as a mural videomap mix. The tag says festival and mapping. The title binds a place, 2 Mai, to the ȘI festival. Tony Macpela's description on the card is deliberately short: "Mural Videomap MIX." A mural is a wall that already has an image. Mapping a mural means negotiating with a picture that is not yours, not inventing a shuttle and not furnishing a kitchen. MIX says more than one visual idea shares that wall. The page stops where the card stops.

Short text is not missing text. Padding it with a fictional program, a lineup, or a claim that he curated the festival would be a doorway trick aimed at the word "festival." The YouTube link is how the mix looks. The neighboring mapping pages show the other available scales: a kitchen with a dance, a UNATC facade as a shuttle, an outdoor Light Up demo. 2 Mai is the mural. If you know ȘI festival as a new-media event on the coast, you already know more context than the card prints. This site will not absorb that outside knowledge into the credit. It will point at the title, the tag, and the video, and let the mural be a mural.
