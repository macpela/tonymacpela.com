VIDEO MAP UNATC

VIDEO MAP UNATC is the facade piece. The portfolio's description is three beats: the building becomes a space shuttle, UFOs pass, the journey continues past Earth. The tag is UNATC, Mapping. Tony does not print a street address or a statement that this was an official university commission beyond that tag, so this page will not promote the tag into a contract. What is solid is the image idea. A facade — a heavy, institutional object — is asked to behave like a vehicle leaving the ground. That is mapping as transformation of architecture, which is the job, as distinct from mapping as decoration of a wall that remains a wall.

The kitchen piece transforms a small domestic volume and ends in a dance. This one transforms a building into departure. The scale of the fiction is the difference a producer can actually use. UFOs and a shuttle are a tone: playful, science-fiction, fast. The giraffes lighting plot is not going to supply that tone, and neither is Efectul. The YouTube link is the record of how literal the shuttle became. Until you watch it, the brief is the three beats and the UNATC tag, nothing more. iMapp and Spotlight already own the generic Romanian query for monumental mapping. This page is for the title and for Tony Macpela's name beside it.
