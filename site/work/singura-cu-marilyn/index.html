Singură cu Marilyn belongs to the AproapE suite: video projection for performative readings of current Hispanic theater, so that contemporary Spanish dramaturgy can catch the stage light. Directed by Bobi Pricop. Tony Macpela's credit, as the homepage has always stated it, is video projection — not laser, not a performer's role, not architectural mapping.
