Actor

Tony Macpela is an actor in Bucharest. The same name signs the laser, the video, and the installations, because the practice is one person doing several jobs, not a brand with a hidden team. Someone who searches Tony Macpela actor and someone who searches Tony Macpela laser should land on different pages that say which job they found. This is the acting page. It does not invent a cast list. The productions linked below are the stage works in the design archive — video, laser, lighting, projection — and they are labeled that way on purpose.

An actor who also builds the picture has a practical advantage in the room, and a practical risk on a CV. The advantage is that he can read a scene from the inside and from the booth: where a body needs to be seen, and what the light will do to that body. The risk is that a director looking for a performer is handed a portfolio of design credits and decides the acting is a slogan. It is not a slogan. It is the other half of the work, and this URL exists so the design archive cannot swallow it. If you are casting, say so when you write. If you are hiring a designer, use the laser, video, interactive, or technical director pages instead of treating this one as a shop window for gear.

The school line printed on the Ars Electronica credit for ASCEND is an MA in Interactive Technologies for Performing and Media Arts at UNATC I.L. Caragiale. That is education, not a role. It explains why acting and interactive systems are allowed to live in one practice without being billed as the same credit. The degree does not add a part to a program, and this page will not pretend that it does.

Dacă cauți un actor în București care înțelege și lumina de spectacol, video mapping-ul și instalația interactivă, pagina asta este locul unde Tony Macpela își asumă meseria de actor. Lista de mai jos nu este un CV de distribuție. Sunt producțiile de teatru pe care le-a desenat — video, laser, lighting, proiecție — și fiecare titlu duce la pagina lucrării, cu regizorul și tipul de credit așa cum sunt publicate.

Bucharest is the city on the contact line. Write to maccpela@gmail.com or call +40 720 906 907. The portrait, the works, and the 2025 festival credit for ASCEND are the public record. Nothing here claims a part he has not printed. The offer is a performer who can talk to a lighting plan, a laser cue, and an interactive system without translating through three other people — and who will still name the job you actually called about.
